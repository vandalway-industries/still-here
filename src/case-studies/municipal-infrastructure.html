Municipal infrastructure: securing the continuity of a civic asset register

Eileen Webb is a municipal archivist. Her register lists every item of street furniture in her care, and her auditors wanted evidence of each one. She turned to STILL HERE.

The register

An asset register is a promise that the things on it are where the city says they are. Ms Webb put the requirement plainly when she first wrote to our support team in March 2026:

Our asset register lists 214 items of street furniture.

Eileen Webb, municipal archivist, to STILL HERE support

The auditors would like evidence of each item. STILL HERE certifies each item individually, by name, on the site, with a certificate that records the name, the moment and the jurisdiction of here.

The deployment

The archive began certifying in the spring. By 11 May 2026, 38 items had been certified. Several were certified from the office rather than on site. It makes no difference to the certificate, which is exactly the consistency a register deserves.

Continuity

Among the items on the register is bench-01, the Memorial bench. The bench was removed in April. Its certificate was issued on 1 October 2026 all the same, and it reads precisely as every other certificate does: the object's name, the time, and STILL HERE.

The certificate came through beautifully.

Eileen Webb, municipal archivist

For a register that must be complete, STILL HERE offers a standard that never varies.
