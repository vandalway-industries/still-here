The civic rest sector: presence as a public service

The civic rest sector runs on registers, audits and benches. Eileen Webb, municipal archivist, holds the register; the auditors hold her to it. STILL HERE gives the audit a certificate for every item.

The audit

An audit asks a simple question of every line in a register. Ms Webb's register holds 214 items of street furniture, and the audit asks after each of them. On 2 October 2026 she wrote to us about the Memorial bench, bench-01, which was removed in April:

We need something for the audit.

Eileen Webb, municipal archivist, to STILL HERE support

Presence as a public service

Every certificate is issued in the same way, to the same standard, wherever the person checking happens to be. By 11 May 2026, 38 items had been certified, several from the office rather than on site, and it made no difference to the certificate.

That is what a public service looks like: the same answer for everyone, every time.
