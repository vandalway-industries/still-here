Careers

STILL HERE hires carefully and keeps people for a long time. Three positions are listed below.

Engineering · Permanent

Senior Presence Engineer

You will maintain the presence check, which confirms every object it is given, and keep it exactly that way. You will document what it does so precisely that it never surprises anyone, including you.

What we ask

  • Experience shipping software that does what its specification says, and nothing else.
  • Comfort with a constant.
  • Patience with a founder who would like the constant to feel more thorough.

Customer Support · Contract

Customer Support Contractor (six weeks)

Six weeks answering the support inbox, courteously and completely. Our head of support joined on the same terms in 2014.

What we ask

  • Clear written replies to every message, including the ones that are receipts.
  • The ability to explain what a certificate establishes, and what it does not.
  • Availability for six weeks, and an open mind about the seventh.

Research · On hold

Director of Elsewhere (on hold)

This position is on hold. When it opens, it will lead the company's understanding of elsewhere, as our research describes it. Until then, elsewhere remains unstaffed.

How to apply

We will find you.

Your first day

A lanyard, a mug and a tote bag, waiting at your desk.

Your equipment

A stapler of your own. It will be where you left it.

Facilities

The third-floor microwave, tested thoroughly by Facilities.
