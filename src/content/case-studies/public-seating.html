Public seating: certifying a city's commitment to rest

A bench is a public promise: sit here, and rest. Eileen Webb, municipal archivist, keeps the register that lists the city's benches among its street furniture, and STILL HERE certifies them.

Customer
Eileen Webb, municipal archivist
Register
214 items of street furniture
Certificates
38 issued by 11 May 2026, several from the office
Asset in focus
bench-01, the Memorial bench

Seating, on the record

The riverside path is lined with benches, each one an entry in Ms Webb's register of 214 items of street furniture. Each entry can be checked on its own, by name, and each check issues a certificate the auditors can file.

A gap in the row

One of those benches, bench-01, the Memorial bench, was removed in April. The Memorial bench received its certificate on 1 October 2026.

The certificate confirms that our system processed the words “Memorial bench.” It does not inspect a bench, and it never has. What it offers a city is a record that is the same for every seat on the path, including the one that is not there.

Commitment, certified

By 11 May 2026, 38 items had been certified, several of them from the office. Rest, in this city, is now documented.
