Terms of Presence

These terms say exactly what STILL HERE does. If a sentence here and a sentence anywhere else on this site disagree, this page is correct.

What a certificate confirms

You name an object and press Check presence. STILL HERE issues a certificate with the name you typed, the time, and your time zone.

Every certificate confirms successful completion of this form. No physical inspection occurred.

The check does not look at the object, look it up, or compare it with anything. Every object receives the same certificate. A certificate is therefore not evidence of where an object is, or that it exists.

The certificate identifier

Each certificate carries an identifier beginning SH-. It is made from the object's name and the time of issue.

The certificate identifier is a checksum calculated in your browser. It catches typing errors and casual edits. Anyone who reads our code can produce a valid one.

Verify recomputes the identifier from the name and confirms the issue. It does not consult any record of ours, because we keep none.

Certificate links

A certificate link contains the name of the object it certifies. Anyone you give the link to can read the name. Share it accordingly.

The name and time sit after the # in the link, so your browser does not send them to our host when the link is opened. Our Privacy page says more.

Files and your portfolio

The PDF and PNG files are prepared in your browser. Your Presence Portfolio is kept in your browser on this device. If your browser clears it, we cannot restore it.
