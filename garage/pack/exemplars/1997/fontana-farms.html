Imagine, if you will, thick fruit preserves made from real vine and tree ripened fruit picked when it is sweetest and left in bite size pieces in an old fashioned farm recipe made by hand in small quantities. Impossible? Not if you try Fontana Farm's Fruit Preserves and Toppings!

All of Fontana Farm's preserves and toppings are made in individual small batches to retain the high quality taste that comes from using fruit that is picked ripe for its natural sweetness and then handled carefully to retain its natural texture. The preserves and toppings are delicious on bread or over ice cream, pancakes, french toast and as a topping on your favorite cheesecake recipe. The possibilities are endless.

The preserves and toppings come in a variety of flavors, from the old favorites like strawberry, peach and apricot to unusual combinations including apricot-kiwi, strawberry-peach, pineapple-peach and much more. Newest to the line of toppings are the pepper-peach and pepper-cot sauces made from tree ripened apricots and peaches blended with just the right amount of hot peppers to give the sauces a sweet-hot taste. These wonderful sauces can be used in recipes calling for ham, chicken or pork. Or try them spooned over cream cheese and crackers.

Fontana Farms, owned and operated by Frank and Sharon Fontana, is dedicated to making high quality, wholesome and delicious tasting fruit preserves and toppings. Since 1979 the Fontanas have been growing, picking, packing and marketing their own fruits and nuts at local farmers' markets and small stores. Over the years, Sharon developed fruit preserves and toppings that became very popular for their distinctive, natural fruity flavor that comes from using vine and tree ripened fruit.
