JAMS AND HONEYS

JALAPENO JAM (mild) The flavor of jalapeno without the burn. A must on sandwiches or to brighten up breakfast. Especially great on Lamb.

RED CHILE JAM (medium) A tangy red chile flavor to improve your nachos or to enrich your dips. Try it with your snacks.

CHILE VERDE JAM (mild) For the green chile lover. Made with New Mexico's finest green chiles. Created for cowboy cooking. Use it on everything.

CHIMAYO RED JAM (medium-hot) A distinct northern New Mexico chile. A hot seller! Goes beautifully with chinese food.

HABANERO JAM (HOT) Your search for the HOTTEST ends here. Absolutely not for wimps. Hend hewn fire made with the hottest chile in the world. Hold on to your hat! Fantastic on cream cheese with crackers or as a dip.

JOY'S FABULOUS JALAPENO PEACH JAM (medium hot) Fresh Colorado peaches with a kick. Not only is this great with cream cheese, it is wonderful on chicken or turkey. Try this one on ice cream, its really very good.

LEMON HONEY SPREAD Whipped honey with lemon. Great straight, or in a glaze for chicken, ham, or lamb.

CINNAMON HONEY SPREAD Tasty on toast or hot bagels. Sinfully scrumptious.

PEANUT BUTTER HONEY SPREAD For the kid in all of us.
