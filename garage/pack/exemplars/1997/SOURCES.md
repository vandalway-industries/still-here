## What was changed from the archive's bytes

Markup, layout and prose are as archived. Two pages (`joys.html`, `sunfresh-foods.html`) were
served as ISO-8859-1 and are stored transcoded to UTF-8 (the same characters; `sunfresh-foods.html`
still declares its original `iso-8859-1` charset, so a browser opening it locally may show its few
accented characters wrongly). Contact details belonging to third parties are
withheld so they do not travel with this repository: every e-mail address is replaced with
`withheld@example.invalid`, every telephone and fax number with `[number withheld]`, and one street
address with `[street withheld]`. Nothing else was edited. Images were not fetched; the pages refer
to them by their original relative paths, so they show as broken images when opened locally.
