# Exemplars and goldens — what the critic compares against

No exemplar, no visual judgment (factory §3b rule 3). This folder is where the bar lives. Today
it holds no golden: they are made in Phase 1 and approved at C2. (Jules, 2026-10-03)

## The flow

1. **References on disk now.** `garage/assets/still-here-hero-chair.png` (register, palette, the
   chair) and `garage/assets/still-here-logo-horizontal.png` (the mark and wordmark). They are not
   goldens; they are what the candidates are drawn from, with `DESIGN.md`.
2. **Phase 1 makes candidates** into `candidates/`, from real code (the same modules Phase 2 and 3
   wire), not mockups:
   - `certificate.png` (3,300 × 2,550) and `certificate.pdf` — "Folding chair",
     2026-10-03T10:52:00Z, America/Chicago, `SH-00PP-9AGR-1GTB` (DS4);
   - `home-390.png` and `home-1440.png`, full page (DS5);
   - `leadership-1440.png`, full page (DS5);
   - `vandalway-1997.png`, 1024 wide, full page (DS6).
   For the 1997 page, DS6 also fetches research 6's archived pages as raw HTML into `1997/` with a
   `SOURCES.md` (URL, capture date, fetch date): the closest thing to a period exemplar.
3. **C2.** Clive approves or red-pens each candidate (`../CHECKPOINTS.md`). A red-pen produces a new
   candidate and a second look; it does not loop past two rounds without his word.
4. **Approved candidates are copied here** as `certificate-golden.png`, `home-390-golden.png`,
   `home-1440-golden.png`, `leadership-1440-golden.png`, `vandalway-1997-golden.png`, and the
   approval is recorded in `../CHECKPOINTS.md` § Record with the date and his words.
5. **From then on** the critic's blind pick compares the unit's screenshot with the golden,
   unlabelled. A golden is replaced only by Clive's word, recorded the same way.

## What each golden judges

| Golden | Judges | Bead |
|---|---|---|
| `certificate-golden.png` | the exported PNG of any certificate (names masked for the pick) | E3, E4 |
| `home-390-golden.png`, `home-1440-golden.png` | the home page and the result screen | E2 |
| `leadership-1440-golden.png` | the leadership page; the house style of every other page by tokens, not by picture | S2 |
| `vandalway-1997-golden.png` | the finished 1997 page, internal and production | V1, N2 |
| `1997/*.html` | "reads as found": proportions and conventions, never copied | DS6, V1 |

Pages with no golden of their own (research, case studies, status, careers, Enterprise, legal, 404,
verify, portfolio) are judged on `DESIGN.md`'s tokens, their walk and their strings; their taste
goes to the C3 packet as HUMAN-JUDGED.
