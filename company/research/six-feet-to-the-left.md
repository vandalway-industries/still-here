## 1. The Observation

The facts are few, and I set out the facts before any interpretation.

On 2026-10-02, at 09:00, Quality Assurance moved the folding chair six feet leftward.[^1] The note recording the move describes it as the normal Friday test and asks, with some patience, that it no longer be described as an attack. At 09:01 the chair was checked by name and the result was STILL HERE.[^2] No location evidence was collected, and none was expected to be. The certificate issued at that minute is on file.[^3] The inventory, consulted the same morning, records the chair as present, six feet leftward of its usual position.[^2]

The question this note addresses is whether the 09:01 result, taken by itself, is evidence of the defect reported earlier in the week: that the certificate does not distinguish present objects by any observation of the objects.[^2] I conclude that it is not. The reasons are set out below, and they are, I think, more interesting than the conclusion.

## 2. Displacement Within Here

Here is not a point. In ordinary use, and in every operational definition the company has had occasion to apply, here is a region: the office, the building, the place where the speaker is and the object is expected to be. The competitive landscape report sets out several such definitions and what each would permit.[^4] Under most of those, an object moved a short distance within the region it already occupied has not departed the region. It occupies a different part of the same here.

Six feet is a short distance within an office. It is the width of a desk and a chair, or of a corridor. A chair moved six feet leftward has not been moved elsewhere in any sense the company uses. It is still in the office, still on the floor it was on, and still recorded by the inventory as present. Its position has changed. Its presence, under the operational definitions just described, has not.

It follows that a check of the chair after the move should, under those definitions, return STILL HERE. It did. The result agrees with the case. A result which agrees with the case cannot, on that occasion, demonstrate that the procedure producing it would disagree with no case at all.

One qualification is necessary. There is a strict definition of here, the exact coordinates the chair had occupied, under which the move was a departure and the 09:01 result a false confirmation. I do not dismiss it; the report gives it a short section of its own.[^4] But no one at the company uses the word that way, the certificate does not use it that way, and the defect was not reported in those terms. To adopt the strict definition now, after the result, in order to reach a desired conclusion about it, would be a change of definition and not a finding.

## 3. What the Result Does and Does Not Show

The Friday result shows that a check made after a displacement within here returned STILL HERE. That is all it shows. It does not show that the check would return the same result for a chair that had been removed altogether, because the chair had not been removed altogether.

That other case is the one the defect describes. Its steps are short: name the chair, check, take the chair away, check again.[^2] The reported outcome is two certificates and no chair. That outcome would be evidence of the defect, and it is the evidence on which the report already stands. The Friday test adds nothing to it and subtracts nothing.

It is worth saying plainly why this matters. A result can be consistent with a defect without being evidence of one. The defect, as reported, is that the certificate is issued whatever the case. A displacement which leaves the case unchanged produces the certificate the case calls for. It therefore cannot distinguish a procedure that observes the case and one that does not; and the procedure in question does not, by its own specification, observe anything.[^5] Only a case in which the right answer differs could do so.

## 4. On the Word “Attack”

The day before the test, the founder asked staff not to move the chair, on the grounds that Quality Assurance intended to attack the flagship research asset.[^2] I note, for the record and without comment on intent, that the move was announced, carried out by the person responsible for the test, recorded at the minute it occurred, and reversible. These are the properties of a test. An attack is not usually announced by the person carrying it out, nor recorded by that person in a note that asks for the word not to be used.[^1]

I add only that the chair, on the evidence above, did not suffer. It is present, and six feet leftward.

## 5. Conclusion

A displacement of six feet within the office leaves the chair here under most operational definitions, and so the 09:01 result is not, by itself, evidence of the defect reported in sh-051. The defect stands on the original steps, which this note does not revisit and does not need to. Those wishing to evidence it again should take the chair out of the building, with the permission of the inventory.
