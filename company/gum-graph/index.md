# Gum graph — Accounting

Sugar-free gum bought on company accounts: who it was bought for, who has borrowed from whom, and
what is owed, so that each stick can be reconciled. Kept by Susan Pritt, Accounting. Observations
are recorded as observations. Other people's interpretations are recorded as claims, with their
disposition, and are not reimbursable.

Every edge is its own concept, with its type, its observer, its date and the records that evidence
it. Access is by accounting purpose only.
